## Reuse and delivery notes

- The Sina album identifies the campus photographs as supplied by Shenyang Ligong University. The page does not state a reusable license; confirm permission with the university before submitting or publishing the entry publicly.
- The education department photograph remains hosted by the government website. Keep its attribution and source link if it is reused.
- These photographs are referenced as remote image URLs; the site needs internet access and the source hosts must allow image loading. For an offline competition package or long-term deployment, replace them with school-approved image files and update this source ledger.
- Every one of the 44 place detail views now has a dedicated image frame. The available point-specific or clearly labeled reference photographs are assigned to Qinhu Lake, Longxiang Stadium, dining locations, Confucius statue, campus gates, and the Weapons Museum. Other places show a named “photo to be added” frame until their own images are supplied; no unrelated picture is presented as a verified location photo.
